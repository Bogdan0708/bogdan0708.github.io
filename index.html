About

I bring 10+ years of operations leadership to applied AI development, with projects grounded in funding, hospitality and municipal workflows.

My operations background spans UK hospitality and EU-funded delivery. In the UK, I led teams as Restaurant Manager at 108 Brasserie (Marylebone Hotel), Hospitality Manager at London Business School (50-person team serving 1,000–2,000 guests/day), General Manager at The Crown (Mosaic Pub & Dining), Assistant GM at Head of the River (Fuller's, Oxford).

I'm also the founder of Mitch from Transylvania, a London food-market business started in early 2025. As General Manager at Taina Lemnului in Romania, I managed EU-funded project delivery, documentation and audit requirements.

Since early 2025, my independent AI project work has focused on turning domain requirements into tested applications. I use deterministic rules for authoritative calculations and explicit review before external actions.
